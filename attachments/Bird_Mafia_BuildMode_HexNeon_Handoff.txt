## Seed logic lock (2026-10-11)

Per operation:
- 9 Hex Seeds (permanent small buffs, one per L1–L9).
- L10 Neon Chaos Seed only appears if that operation’s 9 Hex Seeds are already collected.
- Neon Chaos Seed is temporary: major scaled increase of all Hex buffs for the boss fight only.
- Scale = operation number (Op 1 = 1×, Op 4 = 4×, Op 10 = 10×).
- Following operations have independent Hex sets for the Neon check.
- Permanent Hex stacks stay global.
